# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

Because this project processes images of faces and bodies, never share photos of people in issues or pull requests without their explicit consent.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may ban contributors who violate this code.
